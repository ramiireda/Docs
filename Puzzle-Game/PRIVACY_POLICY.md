# Privacy Policy for Kids Puzzle Game

**Last Updated:** November 1, 2026

**Kids Puzzle Game** ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed as an educational, family-friendly jigsaw puzzle game designed for children and families. We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of our young players and their families.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection, usage, and child safety compliance, including adherence to the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **Google Play Families Policy**.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection

* **We do not collect any personal data.**
* The app does not require account creation, registration, or login.
* We do not collect names, email addresses, physical addresses, telephone numbers, photos, voice recordings, or precise geolocation.
* All game progress (unlocked levels, completed puzzles, and stars earned) is stored locally on the user's device (`user://kids_puzzle_save.cfg`) and is never transmitted to any external server.

---

### 2. Advertising and Child-Directed Treatment (COPPA Compliance)

To keep the game free to play, **Kids Puzzle Game** displays family-friendly rewarded video advertisements (e.g. to provide optional hints when solving puzzles). 

Advertising is served through **Google AdMob**, a Google Play Families Self-Certified Ad Network. We strictly enforce the following child protection settings:

* **COPPA Child-Directed Tag (`tag_for_child_directed_treatment = true`):** All ad requests explicitly instruct Google AdMob to treat the user as a child under COPPA regulations.
* **Under Age of Consent Tag (`tag_for_under_age_of_consent = true`):** Enforces GDPR-K and global child privacy protections.
* **Maximum Ad Content Rating: General Audiences ("G"):** Only ads suitable for young children and general audiences are permitted to display.
* **No Personalized Advertising / No Profiling:** Behavioral tracking, interest-based advertising, and remarketing are strictly disabled. No persistent identifiers are used for cross-app or cross-site tracking.

---

### 3. Permissions Requested

The app only requests standard Android system permissions necessary for gameplay and ad display:
* `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required solely to stream rewarded video ads from Google AdMob and verify connectivity.
* The app does not request access to contacts, storage, camera, microphone, or device sensors.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy Assurance

We do not knowingly contact or collect information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of consent in your country). If a parent or guardian believes that personal information has somehow been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to delete such data.
