Privacy Policy for Kids Puzzle Game

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Summary for Parents: Kids Puzzle Game is designed strictly for children and families. We do NOT collect any personal data, require no accounts, and strictly enforce COPPA and Google Play Families protections for child-safe ads.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

  • We do not collect any personal data.
  • The app does not require account creation, registration, or login.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, or precise geolocation.
  • All game progress and stars are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

2. Advertising & Child-Directed Treatment (COPPA Compliance)

To keep the game free to play, Kids Puzzle Game displays family-friendly rewarded video advertisements to provide optional puzzle hints. Advertising is served through Google AdMob, an official Google Play Families Certified Ad Network, under strict child protection safeguards:

  • COPPA Child-Directed Tag: All ad requests explicitly declare child-directed treatment.
  • Under Age of Consent: Full compliance with GDPR-K and global child privacy standards.
  • Maximum Rating ("G"): Only ads rated suitable for general and young audiences are served.
  • No Behavioral Tracking: Personalized advertising, behavioral profiling, and cross-app tracking are strictly prohibited and disabled.

3. Permissions Requested

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely to stream child-friendly video ads and verify network connectivity.
  • No access to camera, microphone, contacts, location, or device storage is ever requested.

4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated is:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding child safety or this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@kidspuzzlegame.com.
